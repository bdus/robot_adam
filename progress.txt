## Key Learnings
1. planar_move teleports base instead of driving wheels → causes instability
2. diff_drive drives actual wheel joints with physics → smooth and stable
3. Original: all 4 wheels fixed = wheels drag sideways during turns = rollover
4. Solution: rear wheels driven, front wheels free-rolling = proper skid-steer physics
5. Critical fixes:
   - All wheels continuous (no dragging)
   - Wheel friction increased to 1.0 (no slipping)
   - Wheel mass reduced to 0.1kg (lower inertia)
   - Max wheel torque increased to 100Nm (sufficient drive)
   - Fixed robot_base_frame in odom TF
